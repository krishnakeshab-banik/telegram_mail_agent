# Meetings

`/meet` and `/today` list meetings already on the calendar for today, including a join link when the event has one. `/today` also lists deadlines due within 24 hours and replies you still owe.

A meeting found in mail stays a proposal until you confirm it. Confirming creates the Google Calendar event. Nothing on the calendar is created, changed, or deleted without that tap.

Scheduling a new meeting from chat, sending invites, and rescheduling are not in this step. `/free` shows the same today's meetings list as `/meet`.
