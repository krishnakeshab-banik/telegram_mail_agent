# Changelog

## Phase 1

Project skeleton, settings, SQLAlchemy models, Alembic migration, Telegram allowlist, and encrypted Google OAuth storage.

## Phase 2

Gmail history sync, MIME parsing, newsletter pre-filter, Gemini classification, importance adjustment, and Telegram alerts with quiet hours.

## Phase 3

Reply drafts, edit and regenerate, Gmail send with threading headers, approval expiry, and the audit log.

## Phase 4

Meeting extraction, conflict checks, and calendar create, update, and delete only after approval.

## Phase 5

Tasks, deadlines, reminders, follow-ups, morning briefing, and evening wrap-up.

## Phase 6

Attachment parsing for PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, text, and images, thread summaries, categories, and optional Gmail labels.

## Phase 7

Natural-language intent routing and mailbox answers. Owner text is the only input that can request an action.

## Phase 8

Contact profiles, VIP and mute learning, writing-style notes, and `/preferences`.

## Phase 9

Retries, rate limits, prompt-injection tests, Docker, and the setup docs.
